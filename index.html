Summoning the boss

Primal Anthalon does not wait in an arena. Players with the Spawn Primal Anthalon buff can use Offer Corruption to drop a Comet Shard trade pack at the Primal Mana Tower.

Offer Corruption / Drop Comet Shard

Dataskills 9001942, 9001958

"Drop a comet shard pack as an offering to the Primal Mana Tower." Each offering adds a stack of The corruption grows... (max 10 stacks). The land "hungers for primal energy" and the aura gets stronger as you approach the tower.

Spawn Primal Anthalon

Dataskill 9001943

Summons the boss. The tower becomes Corrupted (immune) for 10 minutes. A Primal Anthalon Anchor NPC exists as a teleport point for the boss, matching the buff "Anchor Point: used as an anchor point for Anthalon to teleport to".

DataReset settings: return distance 100m; absolute return distance 200m. Housing areas can trigger a return. The basic attack, Dark Arrow, has a configured 1s cast and 40m range. The final number of offerings required to summon him remains unconfirmed.

Fight flow

The AI uses the generic almighty_npc script with the big-monster skill pattern. The script only starts a skill list; the order, delays and HP gates come from a server-side parameter block that is not in the client. So the order below follows the buffs' own text, not a confirmed rotation.

  • Registered mechanics: pulls and area damage, plus Generals and necromancers. Their opening timing is unconfirmed.
  • Other registered effects: Absorb Mind levitation, Soul marks, Rot infections and chant rituals. Their live activation and cadence are unconfirmed.
  • Eternal Spires: the tooltip names a 10% HP trigger, but the actual HP gate is unconfirmed. Spire protection is registered; destroying all spires to end the phase is an inference.
  • Despawn timer: the Urgent Battle buff says Primal Anthalon disappears when it expires, and its duration is 2 hours (7,200,000 ms). Data
  • Registered enrage chain: expiration of the 5-minute Reign of Oblivion buff grants Godeater. The separate 3-minute channel is a different record; live interruption rules are unconfirmed.

Abilities

Primal Anthalon's Necromantic Flame

Dataskill 9001844, buff 9000846/9000847

The shared Necromantic Flame / Telekinesis plot searches within 100m and pulls up to 200 eligible targets; targets already tagged with Absorb Mind are excluded. The plot telegraphs for 3s, waits 0.5s after location selection, applies a 1s pull, then waits another 0.5s before creating a telekinesis ground area. The separate Flame tooltip lists 15m and 250 to 300 magic damage every second; those values should not be treated as the range and damage of the entire shared plot. A matching doodad ("Necromantic Flame") and an Engulfed in flames debuff exist. Exact live hazard damage, lifetime and cadence remain unconfirmed. Players can also get a one-time "use within 180s" version of the flame from the Primal Kyrios set.

Absorb Mind: Empty Mind

Databuffs 9000990, 9001006, 9001107 to 9001110

The absorption branch begins 6s after Ritual Summoning starts. It searches 100m for eligible targets, excluding bound adds and spires, and applies Primal Levitate plus Empty Mind: Stage 1. Levitation stuns victims, ticks damage and burns mana every second. The damage configuration has a 100 fixed base, charge scaling and a 100% health-steal setting; this setting alone does not establish the live amount healed. The signature mechanic. Primal Levitate makes your body "slowly begin to float" (ticks every second). Absorbing mind and Absorb Mind Activation last 5s. Then Empty Mind marks players whose minds are being drained: they "take increased damage from Absorb Mind as stacks are consumed."

Empty Mind stages generate charges every 0.1s: Stage 1 adds 1, Stage 2 adds 5 and Stage 3 adds 10. Stage 1 is directly linked; later transitions remain unconfirmed. Three stage buffs follow: Stage 1 (up to 5 stacks), Stage 2 (up to 5 stacks) and Stage 3 (1 stack), each described as "increases all damage by 500%". The tooltip does not clearly identify whose damage is increased or whether this means damage dealt or received; do not treat it as a confirmed player damage bonus. Inferred These stages suggest escalation, but later stage transitions and their live outcome are unconfirmed.

The counter is Levitate Dispel (skill 9001941): "dispels Anthalon's Absorb Mind levitation". Primal Gravity Fall (3s) is the boosted-gravity drop that follows.

It is also a healing mechanic. Databuffs 9001152, 9001153, 9001158, 9001160, 9001162, 9001163. The permanent aura Mind Absorption (checks a 300m area every second) says "nearby enemies that have their mind absorbed will heal Anthalon". Mind Condensation (2s) leads into a 10s Mind Absorption buff on Anthalon that heals him every 0.5s and "consumes Absorption charges to increase healing significantly" (the charges last 30s). A Mind Absorption Tick (3s) on a player means "upon death, heals Anthalon". Mind Emptied marks a player who "cannot give additional healing to Anthalon".

Periodic healing and death-triggered healing are distinct: the 10s boss buff heals every 0.5s (44,000 fixed base plus charge scaling), while death healing requires the separate 3s Mind Absorption Tick marker. Absorption charges last 30s. Practical strategy: seek the mechanic that invokes Levitate Dispel and keep marked players alive; this does not establish that an ordinary cleanse works or that every drained death heals him. Inferred

Soul Tether, Soul Weight, Soul Explosion

Databuffs 9000848 to 9000851, 9000882 to 9000884

Soul Tether checks 150m every 0.5s and applies Soul Exposed. Live activation of this branch is unconfirmed. Soul Exposed means that when you die, your soul explodes and debuffs allies: the tooltip says 50m, while the explosion configuration says 200m. This radius discrepancy is unresolved. Soul Weight (300s, max 3 stacks) pulls a unit's soul from its vessel; at three stacks it upgrades and lowers movement speed by 20%. Soul Weight II adds cast speed, III adds attack speed, and Soul Sundered holds all three 20% slows.

Practical read: dying near your group is the danger, so a marked player should step away from allies. Inferred

Rot

Dataskill 9001847, plot "Primal Rot"

A contagion that spreads by proximity. Full breakdown in the next section.

Primal Acceleration

Dataskill 9001944

Deals spell damage to all enemies within 8m, with a chance to snare. The snare duration is reduced by 50% in PvP.

Necromantic Sunder

Dataskill 9001857

"Smashes open a fault line" dealing physical damage to everything in its path and slowing movement by 60%. Allies in the area gain Earth Energy, taking 20% less damage for 7s. This is a General skill, enabled by Rite of the Damned (see Generals).

Ritual Summoning and the chants

Dataskills 9001846, 9001848, 9001858 to 9001862

Anthalon and his necromancers cast five chants in varying orders, shown in the Ritual Summoning buff text. The Ritual Summoning skill is described as "Summon Wraith". Chanting out of order gives Failed Incantation ("the incantation was chanted incorrectly!"). Registered death triggers pass a chanter effect to a player; its application invokes the matching Chant Cast and checks ritual progress. Failed checks apply Failed Incantation for 5s. Ritual Summoning End invokes Levitate Dispel (9001941), followed by Primal Gravity Fall (3s); the final success connection is not fully exposed. Inferred Kill chanters in the displayed order and avoid uncontrolled AoE. Whether this completes the ritual, stops the summon or frees all victims requires the missing server scripts; interruption is not established as a solution.

Mind Destruction

Dataskill 9001849

Described as "Burn Anthalon's Mana". A separate registered skill with a configured 60s cooldown applies a beam-related debuff and is followed by a 210,000–230,000 HP heal. Whether the live AI uses it independently is unconfirmed.

Who is immune to the Disintegration pulse. The tag "Not Applicable for Rot" sits on four buffs: Rot Selection (6s, "patient zero, not eligible to receive Rot"), Necromantic Rot itself, Anthalon's Authority and Primal Spire. Transfer skills remove one Rot-tagged stack from the carrier.

How to play it: an infected player is a 2m hazard for 9 seconds. Spread out so infected players never stand next to others, and do not stack on a marked ally. Deliberately approaching the intended eligible general to transfer Decay is an inferred strategy. Disintegration lasts 90s and its tooltip specifies death at 3 stacks; count accumulated stacks rather than assuming a second hit kills. Inferred

The Eternal Generals

DataFestival of the Eternal Kings has a configured 5s cast and 60s cooldown, and summons Malrec, Thorne, Vaelor and a skeleton wave.

The NPC block around Primal Anthalon includes three named generals. They carry the buff General Of the Dead, which is the "Anthalon Generals" tag the Rot pulse checks for. Each is "bound by Anthalon". Bone Frenzy (10 min) is Anthalon's primal energy surging through them, enabling Rite of the Damned on all nearby generals.

Anthalon's Authority

Databuff 9000919

An aura (40m radius, ticks every second) that terrifies nearby targets afflicted with the Rebirth Trauma debuff, "preventing them from engaging in a normal battle". The initial Authority effect also dispels a glider-tagged effect.

Ancient Necromancy (12s): "Anthalon's power festers through the Eternal General. Upon expiration, the corruption will grant the general immense power." Necromatic Affliction (60s) reduces physical and magic defense by 30%. Data The 25m distance is general-to-general buff sharing; the 30m distance is the resulting defense-reduction aura affecting enemies. Inferred Keeping generals more than 25m apart should prevent shared buffs, but does not imply that a general's own effects disappear or that players beyond 25m avoid the 30m aura. Inferred

Registered ritual/spire cleanup of generals. Databuffs 9001147 to 9001150, 9001177. Anthalon Ritual and Anthalon: Spires tick every 2s over a very wide radius (300 in the data) and give generals Anthalon's Authority: Ritual or Primal Spires. After 5s each one "deletes generals nearby" through five kill-without-corpse effects. Inferred These records support general cleanup associated with ritual and spire states. They do not establish the precise live phase boundary, ritual-end timing, or a fixed earlier-only window for generals and Decay.

Five more necromancers (Zareth, Voris, Maltheon, Valthek, Theryn) exist in the same ID range. Their chant death triggers are registered (see Ritual Summoning); exact live completion outcomes are not confirmed.

Spires and the enrage

Eternal Spires (HP trigger unconfirmed)

Databuff 9001077

The tooltip says "At 10% HP, triggers eternal spires", but the missing server-side HP gate prevents confirming that threshold. Four named spires, Decay, Despair, Ruin and Oblivion, emit auras that "greatly empower Anthalon". There is also a Spire of Omen and a Primal Spire.

Spire Empowerment and Primal Resurgence

Databuffs 9000995, 9001078 to 9001081, 9001099

Data Spire auras extend 200m and grant Spire Empowerment, described as 99% incoming-damage reduction. Primal Resurgence adds resistance to all damage. Inferred Prioritize protective spires. Destroying every spire to remove protection, release the root or cancel the enrage is a proposed strategy; the phase-exit condition is not exposed.

Reign of Oblivion

Databuff 9001083, 300s

"Anthalon is channeling the primal energy through the spires... upon expiration, Anthalon will annihilate all who he touches." On expiration, this 5-minute buff grants Godeater. A separate 3-minute Reign of Oblivion channel exists; it is a different record. Impending Doom... (180s) and Root Anthalon both reduce his turn speed by 200.

Godeater

Databuff 9001100

"Anthalon's power surges with primal energy! Upon dealing damage, instantly kills the target." The registered damage-trigger effect adds a 999,999 damage hit, effectively a lethal enrage in ordinary circumstances. A separate record, Primal Anthalon's Annihilation, describes his death: "Anthalon dies. Perhaps he has ceased to exist... or he is waiting in another realm."

DataThe Eternal Spires skill has a configured 10-minute cooldown, roots Anthalon, invokes six spawner effects and searches within 200m for an anchor to reposition him. These effects do not establish when the live AI invokes the skill.

Primal Kyrios set

The set shares the boss's theme. Pieces: hood, shirt, sleeves, gloves, sash, pants and shoes.

  • 4-piece: increases Toughness, with a low chance to trigger Anthalon's Necromantic Flame when you take damage.
  • 7-piece: reduces damage taken, with a low chance to trigger Primal Anthalon Mind Absorb when you deal damage.

What is not known

  • The skill order, delays and HP gates. The AI points to parameter block 9000039, which is server-side only.
  • Exact HP, damage numbers for most skills, and loot tables.
  • The purported 10% HP spire trigger, later Empty Mind stage transitions, ritual success outcomes, and the conditions that remove spire protection, release Anthalon or cancel the enrage.
  • Who applies Rot Selection, the final server-side ritual success connection, and what Primed Out of My Mind is. It is described only as "!!!" and adds +1,000,000 to one attribute, which looks like an internal or test buff.

Raid priorities — inferred strategy: separate generals beyond their 25m sharing range; isolate Rot carriers; follow the active chant order; avoid absorption deaths; leave telekinesis hazards; prioritize protective spires. These priorities do not confirm a fixed phase order or completion condition.
